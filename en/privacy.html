Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

1. Overview

Contact+ ("the App") is designed to sync contact photos between Telegram and the iPhone address book. We respect your privacy and strive for maximum transparency.

2. Data Processed

The app processes the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Contacts — names and phone numbers from the iPhone address book, used for matching with Telegram.
  • Telegram data — names, phone numbers, and profile photos of your Telegram contacts.
  • Telegram session — authorization data for interacting with the Telegram API.

3. Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device.
  • The Telegram database is encrypted with a key stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • The match cache is protected by iOS Data Protection (Complete Protection).
  • The usage counter syncs via iCloud Key-Value Store (only the number of updated photos).

4. Third-Party Sharing

The app does not share your data with third parties. We have no servers, analytics, ad SDKs, or trackers.

The app communicates only with the Telegram API (for authorization, contact import, and photo download) directly from your device.

5. Purchases

The app offers a one-time purchase for unlimited access via Apple In-App Purchase. All transactions are handled by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

6. Your Rights

  • You can revoke contacts access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • You can log out of the Telegram session in the app — all local data will be deleted.
  • Deleting the app completely removes all associated data from the device.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions, reach out via the support page.
