Contact+

Telegram photos for your iPhone contacts

Automatically finds your contacts on Telegram and sets their up-to-date profile photos in your address book. Compare photos, choose the ones you like, and update them all at once.

Features

Automatic Matching

Imports your contacts into Telegram, finds matches by phone number, and shows you a list of pairs — all automatically.

Photo Comparison

See the current address book photo and the Telegram photo side by side. You decide — update or keep.

Batch Sync

Select multiple contacts and update their photos with one tap. Or swipe through cards one by one.

Smart Filter

Contacts with matching photos are hidden automatically — you only see the ones that differ.

Security

Your data never leaves the device. The Telegram session is encrypted, the database is protected with a Keychain key. No servers, no analytics, no tracking.
